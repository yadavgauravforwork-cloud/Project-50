# Goa Event Hub

Goa Event Hub is a comprehensive event planning and rental platform based in Goa. We provide a wide range of services including luxury villas, yachts, car rentals, event decor, DJ & sound systems, catering, and more.

## Features

- **50+ Rental Services**: Everything you need for an event in North or South Goa.
- **Popular Packages**: Specially curated bundles for weddings, bachelor parties, birthdays, and corporate offsites.
- **Real-time Search**: Easily find the services you need.
- **Quick Enquiry**: Get an itemized quote within 30 minutes via our contact form or WhatsApp.

## How It Works

1. **Tell Us Your Plan**: Share your event details.
2. **Get a Quote**: Receive a detailed breakup of costs.
3. **Lock Your Date**: Pay a 30% advance to secure your booking.
4. **Enjoy**: Our team handles the setup and management.
